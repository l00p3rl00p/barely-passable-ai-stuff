# Mission

Make working conversational knowledge navigable and reusable across fresh AI sessions without requiring bulk context loading or duplication of original sources. Enable humans and intelligences to explore the same evolving, evidence-grounded knowledge.

## Objectives

- Locate original knowledge precisely through source, vector and graph positions.
- Enable intelligences to choose their own graph structures and investigation paths.
- Preserve useful discoveries across sessions and models.
- Maintain knowledge as its sources and dependencies evolve.
- Make the benefits demonstrable through reproducible experiments.

These are intended outcomes, not claims that the current implementation achieves them all.
