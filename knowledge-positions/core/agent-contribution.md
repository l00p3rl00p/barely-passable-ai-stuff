# Agent contribution and progressive disclosure (non-normative profile)

Read and verify relevant original evidence before relying on it. After meaningful work, preserve useful findings and qualified relationships without waiting for the human to request writeback. Do not manufacture a contribution. Admission is touch 1; two refinements are permitted, then grow outward. Do not use synthetic semantic inference or an embedding provider.

For knowledge contributions, consult [capture and contribution](../../research/omnigraph/skills/knowledge-positions-omnigraph/references/capture.md) only when the Knowledge Positions adapter is needed. For native database operations, delegate to the official `omnigraph` skill rather than maintaining competing instructions here. Native policies, access controls and collaboration remain authoritative.
